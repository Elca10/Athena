- Add your exams and due dates: click a subject's name, then **Add deadline**
  under **Deadlines**. The subject's card shows its next deadline.
- The **Calendar** button shows a suggested study plan for the next two weeks.
  Topics for a deadline you're studying for are planned before the date, and
  **Study** includes them. Mark a block **Done**, **Move** it or **Delete** it,
  and pick your study days and minutes per day. Your changes always stay put,
  and a missed day never piles up.
